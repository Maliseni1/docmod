DocMod

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This describes what DocMod (operated by Chiza Labs) actually collects and does with it, based on how the service is built today. It's written in good faith to be accurate and specific rather than generic, but it isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer if you need a formal compliance assessment.

1. The short version

DocMod doesn't have user accounts, doesn't store the files you convert after the conversion finishes, and doesn't sell data to anyone. It uses a privacy-conscious analytics tool (PostHog) that's configured to never see your filenames or file content - only which category of file and which target format you used.

2. Your files

When you upload a file to convert, it's sent to DocMod's backend, processed to produce the converted output, and the result is returned to you in the same request. Both the uploaded file and the converted file are deleted from the server immediately after the response is sent. They are never written to any database, backup, or log, and no one reviews their content as part of normal operation.

This is handled by PostHog for conversion/error events and Vercel Web Analytics for page views. Both assign an anonymous identifier to your browser session rather than anything tied to your real identity. If your browser or an extension blocks third-party analytics scripts, DocMod still works - it just won't record those events, which is also why some visitors won't see any effect from this section at all.

4. Cookies and local storage

PostHog may set a small amount of local storage or a cookie to remember an anonymous session identifier between page loads. DocMod also registers a minimal browser service worker (sw.js if you're familiar with the term) that caches exactly one static offline page, so the site can show something useful if you lose your connection - this cache has nothing to do with tracking and isn't used to identify you.

5. Who else sees this data

DocMod runs on infrastructure provided by Vercel (frontend hosting and web analytics) and Render (backend processing). PostHog processes the conversion/error analytics described above. These providers act as infrastructure/processors for running the service - none of them receive your file content, since it's never sent to the analytics layer in the first place.

6. Data retention

Uploaded and converted files: deleted immediately after each request, as described above. Analytics events: retained according to PostHog's and Vercel's own retention settings for this project, which may be adjusted over time.

7. Children's privacy

DocMod isn't directed at children, and knowingly doesn't collect personal information from anyone under 13.

8. Your rights and choices

Since DocMod has no accounts and doesn't retain your files, there's no stored personal profile to request a copy of or delete. For the anonymous analytics described above, you can block it entirely via your browser's tracking-protection settings or an ad blocker - the app degrades gracefully either way. If you have a specific question about data associated with your use of DocMod, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
